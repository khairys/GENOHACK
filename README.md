# GENOHACK 2026: Deep-Tech Genomics & Life Sciences Commercialization Repository (DriveKit)

Welcome to the **GENOHACK 2026 DriveKit Repository**. This repository contains complete, high-fidelity Markdown transcriptions of all official curriculum, guidelines, hackathon challenges, workshop materials, spreadsheets, and regulatory frameworks from the **GENOHACK 2026 Deep-Tech Hackathon** hosted by Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) and PathGen.

The theme of this national initiative is:  
> **"Dari Riset Laboratorium Menuju Kemandirian Alat Kesehatan, Obat, dan Ketahanan Pangan Nasional"**  
> *(From Laboratory Research Towards National Self-Reliance in Medical Devices, Pharmaceuticals, and Food Security)*

## 🎯 The Three Competition Tracks

1. **Track 1: Precision Diagnostics**  
   *Focus:* Novel molecular assays, qPCR kits, NGS targeted panels, liquid biopsy, oncology diagnostics, pharmacogenomics, infectious disease monitoring.  
   *Industry Sponsor:* PathGen Diagnostic Nusantara.
2. **Track 2: Food Security & Biodiversity Genomics**  
   *Focus:* Crop disease detection, climate-resilient marker identification, livestock genetics, aquaculture pathogen surveillance, Indonesian endemic biodiversity bioprospecting.  
   *Featured Team:* **Exastra** (Bramantyo Kunni Nurrisqi, Agathan Khairy, Bowo Laksono, Bagus Athallah).
3. **Track 3: Biomanufacturing & Synthetic Biology**  
   *Focus:* Recombinant proteins, microbial cell factories, metabolic pathway engineering, bioreactor scale-up, bioleaching, green chemical biocatalysis.
