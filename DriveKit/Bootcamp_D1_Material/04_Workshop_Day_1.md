## 2. Workshop Day 1 Deliverables & Working Canvas

During Workshop Day 1, each team was tasked with completing three mandatory working templates:

### Exercise 1: Technology–Problem Fit Hypothesis
- Formulate a falsifiable problem statement for the chosen track:
  - *Who specifically suffers from the problem?*
  - *What is the observable negative outcome today?*
  - *Why does the status quo fail to solve it?*
  - *Where does your proposed genomics/synthetic biology solution offer an unfair, 10x defensible advantage?*

### Exercise 2: Market Segmentation Matrix (Worksheet 1.2)
- Identify at least 3 candidate market segments for your core technology.
- Populate the 17-point segmentation wireframe (Task, Benefit, Urgency, Willingness to Pay, Lead Customers, Competition, Partner Dependencies).
- Down-select to **one single Beachhead Market** using the 8 MIT criteria.

### Exercise 3: User Persona & SMUB Canvas
- Create a concrete persona profile:
  - Role, daily workflow, and critical bottlenecks.
  - The Triad breakdown: Name the End User, the Economic Buyer, and the Decision Maker.
- Execute the SMUB framework:
  - **S (Siapa):** Specific user.
  - **M (Masalah):** High-priority friction.
  - **U (Uji):** Planned validation test / pilot.
  - **B (Bukti):** Quantitative success metric (KPI).
