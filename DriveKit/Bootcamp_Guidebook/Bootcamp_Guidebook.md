## Executive Overview & Program Architecture

The **GENOHACK 2026 Bootcamp Guidebook** is the master reference manual and operational workbook for all participating multidisciplinary teams. It integrates the 24-step **Disciplined Entrepreneurship (DE24)** methodology developed by Bill Aulet at MIT, adapted specifically for deep-tech translational genomics, medical devices, bioinformatics, and synthetic biology.

## Complete Day 1 Worksheets: Disciplined Entrepreneurship Steps 1–9 (Pages 11–35)

### Worksheet 1.1: Market Segmentation Matrix Row Definitions (Pages 12–13)
The 18 criteria required to evaluate each potential market segment:

| Row # | Evaluation Dimension | Definitional Scope & Guiding Prompt |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Market Segment** | Carefully named distinct group of potential customers with common characteristics. |
| **2** | **End User** | The individual human who ultimately operates, applies, or interacts directly with the solution. |
| **3** | **Application** | The specific clinical, industrial, or agricultural task or workflow being accomplished. |
| **4** | **Benefits** | The primary tangible economic, clinical, or speed benefit realized by the end user. |
| **5** | **Lead Customers** | Influential lighthouse customers who are innovative and will be respected by peers. |
| **6** | **Market Characteristics** | Macro growth rate, structural trends, willingness to adopt new technologies. |
| **7** | **Partners / Players** | Complementary organizations, suppliers, or distributors needed to deliver the whole product. |
| **8** | **Size of Market** | Estimated number of potential customers multiplied by annual purchase capacity (TAM). |
| **9** | **Competition** | Current status quo alternatives, domestic players, and global incumbents. |
| **10**| **Complementary Assets**| Secondary equipment, software, or certifications required to make the solution functional. |
| **11**| **Frequency of Purchase**| Transaction cadence: one-off capital sale, monthly consumables, or annual subscription. |
| **12**| **Unit Economics** | Estimated gross margin percentage and profitability profile per customer transaction. |
| **13**| **Technological Fit** | Alignment with the venture's current scientific capabilities and core laboratory IP. |
| **14**| **Regulatory Constraints**| Specific clearance pathways (BPOM, Kemenkes AKD/AKL, Halal, CPOB, BSL rating). |
| **15**| **Barriers to Entry** | Intellectual property defensibility, switching costs, or network effects protecting the market. |
| **16**| **Funding Availability** | Existence of capital grants, venture investors, or government procurement funds. |
| **17**| **Cultural / Behavioral Fit**| Degree of behavioral change required from users compared to established cultural habits. |
| **18**| **Personal Alignment** | Founder passion, ethical mission alignment, and long-term team commitment. |

### Worksheet 02.2: Beachhead Market Selection Framework (Page 15)
Evaluate candidate segments against the 7 Strategic Beachhead Tests (Score 1–5):

```markdown
1. Is the target customer adequately funded to purchase?           [ Score: 1 - 5 ]
2. Is the customer readily accessible to our direct sales team?     [ Score: 1 - 5 ]
3. Does the customer have a compelling reason to buy immediately?   [ Score: 1 - 5 ]
4. Can we deliver a complete product with our existing partners?   [ Score: 1 - 5 ]
5. Is entrenched competition weak or vulnerable in this segment?   [ Score: 1 - 5 ]
6. If we win this segment, can we easily leverage into adjacencies? [ Score: 1 - 5 ]
7. Is this market consistent with the values and mission of founders?[ Score: 1 - 5 ]

TOTAL BEACHHEAD SCORE: [ _____ / 35 ]
```

### Worksheet 04.1–04.5: Total Addressable Market (TAM) Worksheets (Pages 17–22)

#### Top-Down TAM Calculation (Worksheet 04.1 & 04.2):
$$	ext{TAM} = 	ext{Total Potential Customers in Beachhead} 	imes 	ext{Annual Average Spending per Customer}$$

```markdown
Target Countable Units (e.g., Hospitals, Testing Labs):    _____________ Units
Average Annual Purchase Frequency (Runs / Kits per Year):  _____________ Runs / Year
Price per Unit / Run:                                      Rp __________ / Run
TOP-DOWN ESTIMATED TAM:                                    Rp __________ / Year
```

#### Bottom-Up TAM Sanity Check (Worksheet 04.5):
* Countable Customer Unit: ______________________________________________________
* How many individual buying units can you realistically contact, pitch, and service in Year 1 to Year 3?
* Benchmark Healthy TAM Range: **Rp 100 Miliar to Rp 1 Triliun ($5M – $50M)**. Below Rp 100M is too small for venture scale; above Rp 1T indicates the beachhead is too broad and unfocused.

### Worksheet 6.1: Full Life Cycle Use Case (Page 27)
Mapping the 10 sequential operational touchpoints of the Persona:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    S1["1. Need"] --> S2["2. Find"] --> S3["3. Analyze"] --> S4["4. Acquire"] --> S5["5. Pay"]
    S5 --> S6["6. Install"] --> S7["7. Use"] --> S8["8. Value"] --> S9["9. Buy More"] --> S10["10. Tell Others"]
```

```markdown
Stage 1: How does the Persona first realize they have a clinical or operational problem?
Stage 2: How do they discover our solution exists?
Stage 3: How do they evaluate and verify our clinical analytical claims?
Stage 4: What hospital procurement or administrative paperwork is required to order?
Stage 5: How is payment authorized and disbursed?
Stage 6: How is the assay/software installed and integrated into lab SOPs?
Stage 7: How do lab staff routinely run tests and generate reports?
Stage 8: How does the Persona experience and measure the value created?

Stage 10: How do they advocate and refer our solution to peer institutions?
```
