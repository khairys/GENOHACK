# Customer discovery worksheet

## Interview target

Livestock-genetics researchers, population geneticists, breeding-center analysts, and laboratory leads who have recently used SNP genotypes for breed or population work. Interviewers should not pitch the tool before hearing the participant's workflow.

## Opening and consent

Explain that this is a student research prototype, not an assay or breeding recommendation. Ask permission to take notes. Do not record names, animal identifiers, unpublished genotypes, or confidential procurement details without explicit consent.

## Questions

1. Please walk me through the last project where your team selected or reviewed genetic markers.
2. What decision did that analysis need to support, and who made the final decision?
3. Where did the data, reference panel, and marker list come from?
4. What took the most time or caused rework? What happened as a result?
5. How do you decide a marker panel is good enough? Which metrics and external checks matter?
6. How do you check whether the panel works for a local breed or subpopulation?
7. What tools, services, or manual steps do you use today? What do they cost in analyst time or project spend?
8. Who controls the budget, data access, and approval to try a new workflow?
9. What evidence would you require before reviewing a panel candidate from an external tool?
10. Could we agree on a concrete next step, such as a review of this report or a scoped analysis using a non-sensitive public cohort?

## Evidence rules

Count only interviews actually completed. A stated interest is not a purchase commitment. A review invitation is a next step, not an LOI. Report verbatim quotes only with permission. If a participant declines, record the role and reason only if they consent.
