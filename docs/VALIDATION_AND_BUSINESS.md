# Validation, customer discovery, and adoption plan

## Current evidence status

At the start of implementation there was no customer interview, pilot, purchase commitment, or assay validation in the repository. Public genomic data can support a computational prototype but cannot establish workflow fit, willingness to pay, clinical/field impact, or laboratory performance. Record any new human validation with the date, role, exact question, observed behavior, and permission to quote.

## Discovery sprint

Interview 3–5 livestock-genetics researchers or laboratory analysts. Ask them to walk through their most recent marker-panel design, time and cost spent, acceptance criteria, procurement path, and evidence required before using a candidate panel. Show the prototype after hearing their current workflow. Seek one specific next action such as reviewing an anonymized report or defining a pilot cohort. Do not describe an interview as an LOI or customer validation.

## Beachhead and business model hypothesis

Beachhead: Indonesian livestock genetics research groups and laboratories that already analyze breed/population genotypes. Initial offer hypothesis: a project-based panel-design and evidence-report service, with software access considered only after repeated workflows are observed. Buyer may be the PI or laboratory head; users are geneticists and analysts; procurement and data owners may be separate.

## Economics to measure

TAM should be built bottom-up as eligible institutions × relevant projects per institution per year × validated annual spend per project. Do not insert unsupported institution counts or prices. Measure analyst and review hours, compute, support, assay validation cost, gross margin, sales cycle, and repeat project rate before estimating LTV or COCA. Mark every input as measured, quoted, or assumed.

## 12-month validation path

| Quarter | Milestone | Evidence to unlock next step |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Independent scientific review; interview researchers; secure a larger cohort | Review notes, dataset permissions/provenance, agreed success criteria |
| Q2 | Group-aware external replication and population coverage analysis | Frozen protocol, held-out cohort report, reproducible analysis |
| Q3 | Laboratory feasibility and assay design with a partner | Probe review, wet-lab call-rate/reproducibility, measured unit cost |
| Q4 | Prospective pilot scoped with a research or breeding institution | Signed pilot scope, owner, cohort, budget path, and success measures |

The prototype is research-use-only. Any future deployment for animal breeding or certification needs domain review, valid population evidence, and fit-for-purpose assay validation.
